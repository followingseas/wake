### Added

- **Choose how the sidebar is sorted** — a button next to the search box switches between recent activity and name. Name order ignores case and compares numbers by value, so `repo2` comes before `repo10`. Repositories and the worktrees and subfolders inside them sort together, and your choice is kept across restarts. Session lists always stay in recent order

### Fixed

- **Nothing happened when the sort setting could not be saved** — Wake now tells you, and the list stays as it was
